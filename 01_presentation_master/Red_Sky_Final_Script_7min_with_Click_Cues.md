## 3. Rehearsal Checkpoints & Clicker Discipline

1. **One Click per Blank Line Gap:** Each blank line in the script corresponds to exactly one click on your clicker.
2. **Never Double-Click:** If an animation entrance is smooth, do not panic and double-click.
3. **If You Advance Ahead:** Keep speaking your current line; your spoken words carry the narrative.
4. **Slide 10 Reminder:** Only **two clicks** are on Slide 10 (each click drops 4 cards simultaneously).
5. **Slide 12 Reminder:** Slide 12 is **100% static**; clicking again will advance to the Backup Directory.

## 4. WORST CASE: When a Teammate Runs Long (With Clicker Guidance)

### 4.1 The Rules
1. **The next speaker never recaps and never apologises.** Start with the first bold sentence of your slide, immediately.
2. **Cut sentences, not slides**, until Level 4. Plain-text sentences are the cuts.
3. **A timekeeper holds up fingers at each gate:** 0 = on time, 1 = Level 1, 2 = Level 2, 3 = Level 3, 4 = Panic path.
4. **Click fast if speaking bold-only:** Advance through the clicks briskly so the screen matches your abbreviated speech.

## 6. Q&A Pocket Card (One Line Each)

| If asked | Say |
|---|---|
| Why not an app? | 20M+ keypad users have no app store, apps can be uninstalled, and only the network sees couriers, shops and vendors. |
| Does Stealth Number protect criminals? | No. It hides you from strangers, not courts: a High Court warrant unmasks it, with two NTL officers approving. |
| Where do dispute savings come from? | Modeled assumptions: estimated complaint volume and a loaded cost per call. Even with zero churn benefit it pays back in under three years. |
| Why 17.5% churn and 1.2M elite? | Planning assumptions for the high-value segment. It is our softest number, which is why we show the zero-churn case. |
| Is the finance NTL's real data? | No. It is a modeled estimate on public assumptions and NTL's 58.6M base. |
| Who pays for refunds? | The developer's 14-day escrow first; a small refund buffer sits in our costs. |
| A child can use an adult's SIM. | Age proof confirms the SIM owner is an adult; Child-Shield and default zero-profiling cover children on shared lines. |
| Is the DPO's veto in the law? | The law requires an independent officer. The sign-off power is NTL policy. |
| Erasure versus 24-month retention? | Split by purpose: commercial profiles are erased within 24 hours; call records sit 24 months in a sealed vault opened only by High Court warrant. |
| Can the gateway miss personal data or stop every prompt injection? | It greatly reduces exposure, backed by logging and the egress filter. No one can fully block injection. |
| Why would shops adopt Flexiload? | Customers will ask for it, and a 25,000-kiosk pilot tests adoption before Phase 2 money is released. |
| How long to roll out? | Four phases over 24 months; Phase 2 funding only if the pilot hits its targets. |
