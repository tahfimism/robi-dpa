## 📋 The Official Case Study (`case.md`): Overview & Mandate

To evaluate the presentation deck properly, you must understand the exact enterprise crisis and consulting assignment established in the competition case study (`case.md`):

### 1. Company Profile & Cybersecurity Baseline
* **New Telecom Ltd (NTL):** One of Bangladesh's leading telecom operators, serving **58.6 Million subscribers** for over 20 years. It manages vast volumes of subscriber identity records, billing information, communication metadata, digital payment trails, cellular tower location data, and digital platform telemetry.
* **The Maturity Baseline:** NTL is not starting from zero. It has a dedicated **Cyber Security and Data Privacy Division**, an international standards-aligned Privacy Governance Framework (ISO/IEC 27001/27701), Privacy-by-Design with least-privilege Zero Trust access, continuous monitoring, and ongoing employee training (`case.md`, lines 21–80).

### 2. The New Reality & The AI Attack Surface
NTL is accelerating its digital transformation by integrating Artificial Intelligence across customer service, operations, and business analytics (`case.md`, lines 87–152). 
The case mandates governance for **Six AI-Powered Systems Under Consideration**:
1. **AI-Enabled Customer Support Assistants**
2. **Intelligent Fraud Detection Systems**
3. **Customer Churn Prediction Models**
4. **Marketing Recommendation Engines**
5. **Network Optimization using Machine Learning**
6. **Automated Document Processing (e-KYC)**

* **The Emerging AI Threats:** Cybercriminals using AI to bypass identity verification; prompt injection attacks (OWASP LLM01) manipulating LLM responses; model inversion attacks (OWASP LLM06) extracting private training data; unmonitored cross-border telemetry leaks to third-party overseas AI vendors; and algorithmic bias in automated decision-making.

### 3. The New Regulatory Environment: Bangladesh PDPA
The Parliament of Bangladesh enacted the **Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA)**, establishing statutory duties for lawful processing, data minimization, accountability, processor compliance, and citizen rights (access, correction, portability, erasure).
* **The Absolute Ban on Tracking Children:** The PDPA strictly prohibits targeted advertising, tracking, profiling, or behavioral monitoring directed at minors under 18 (`case.md`, lines 199–200).

### 4. The Core Challenge & Management's Eight Strategic Concerns
Privacy activities remain fragmented across IT, Legal, Marketing, and Customer Care. Personal data flows through unmapped systems. During an internal review, senior management raised **Eight Pointed Strategic Questions** (`case.md`, lines 227–270):
1. *Where is every piece of customer personal data stored?*
2. *Which applications process sensitive information of customers?*
3. *Which AI systems have access to customer data?*
4. *Which vendors receive customers' personal information?*
5. *What customer data leaves Bangladesh?*
6. *Are all AI-enabled decisions explainable?*
7. *Can the organization demonstrate PDPA compliance during a regulatory audit?*
8. *Who is accountable for privacy decisions across business units?*

### 5. The Consultant Assignment & Evaluation Criteria
Assume you are appointed as a **Data Privacy Consultant** advising NTL's Executive Management. You must deliver a proposal addressing:
* **Problem Understanding (15%):** Deconstructing the telecom and AI crisis.
* **Customer Centricity (30%):** Designing privacy that works for real citizens, including the 20M+ button phone users.
* **Innovation & Technical Excellence (20%):** Carrier-grade network engineering and cryptographic defenses.
* **Strategic Impact & Sustainability (25%):** An audited business case (+BDT 35.5 Cr Net EBITDA) and 24-month roadmap.
* **Presentation & Communication (10%):** Clear, executive-grade storytelling.
