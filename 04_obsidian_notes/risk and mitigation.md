
|Hidden Risk|Why It's Easy to Miss|Our Fix|
|---|---|---|
|Privacy rules make our fraud and churn models use less detailed data — which could quietly make them less accurate, undercutting the savings we're counting on|We're funding the project partly with a number our own privacy rules make harder to hit|Test model accuracy under the new data limits before locking in the financial targets|
|Hiding customer numbers from couriers also hides the number delivery firms use to catch repeat fraud or abusive buyers|We're removing a fraud tool from our partners, not just protecting customers|Give delivery partners a trust score instead, so they keep a signal without seeing the real number|
|The free replacement number for "leaked" numbers is easy to fake-claim — to dodge a debt collector, an ex, or just get a nicer number|The feature creates a new way to exploit us that didn't exist before|Only trigger the free swap from leaks we've verified ourselves, never an unverified customer claim|
|Teams inside NTL whose bonuses depend on ad targeting or third-party billing have a quiet reason to slow-walk the parts of this plan that cut into their numbers|Internal resistance can look like delay, not sabotage, so it's easy to miss|Update those teams' targets before launch, so no one internally benefits from the plan failing|
|Child-Shield only works if a parent knows to turn it on — but the parents who don't know are exactly whose kids are being profiled right now|The fix depends on the awareness the problem proves people don't have|Make basic child protection the default on every line, not something a parent has to discover|
|Voice consent for illiterate users is the one part of the system not backed by hardware — as voice-cloning gets cheaper, it becomes the easiest piece to fake|Everything else is cryptographically secure; this one feature quietly isn't|Pair voice consent with a second simple check, like a callback confirmation, before it counts as final|
|The instant "someone's opening a SIM on your ID" alert is great for catching fraud, but could also tip off an abusive partner watching a victim's phone|A safety feature for one person can become a surveillance tool against another|Let people quietly register a second, private alert number not visible on a shared phone|
|The 2x refund guarantee on unauthorized charges can be gamed — a customer and a shady developer fake a charge and split the refund|We built an anti-fraud feature that can itself be used to commit fraud|Flag unusual refund patterns per device before auto-approving the payout|
|Deleting commercial data within 24 hours protects privacy, but can also delete evidence banks or police need to investigate a fraud claim before a warrant arrives|Fast erasure, built to protect victims, can work against victims in an active case|Hold a short, encrypted grace window for cases already flagged as active fraud|
