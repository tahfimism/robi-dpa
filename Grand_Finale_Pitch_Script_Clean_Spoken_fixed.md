## Authoritative Q&A Defense (30 Seconds Each, Verbatim)

### CFO — "Where does your payback come from?"
> *"Judges, early scoping papers modeled a theoretical ceiling of 160 to 225 crore if all 1.2 million elite subscribers were at immediate risk. As disciplined capital stewards, we rebuilt our model with audited conservatism: applying an industry-standard 17.5% annual churn, a 55% margin, and all operational deductions. That yields +BDT 30.6 Crore Net EBITDA in Year 3, +BDT 104.2 Crore cumulative net cash, and a rock-solid 26-month payback."*

### CFO — "What if churn reduction fails completely?"
> *"Our model does not depend on churn. We ran the Zero-Churn Hurdle Test: setting churn savings to zero. Deflecting 7.9 million call center disputes and commercializing CAMARA APIs alone delivers full CapEx payback in 38.8 months (~39 months) and generates +BDT 21.4 Crore cumulative net cash. Churn defense is pure uncounted upside."*

### CTO — "A 6-digit Flexiload token will collide during evening rush hour."
> *"The token is not a flat global number. It is evaluated in our in-memory Redis cluster as a scoped compound key: Retailer Agent MSISDN plus the 6-digit token. Across 250,000 retail kiosks, concurrency drops to 0.4 active tokens per namespace during peak 600 TPS load, reducing collision probability to less than 0.0001%."*

### CTO — "Can a basic SIM compute zero-knowledge proofs?"
> *"No, and we never ask it to. The SIM executes a standard elliptic curve signature in under 45ms; our central Tier-IV hardware security module computes the Groth16 zero-knowledge proof. For older legacy SIMs without cryptographic coprocessors, the system gracefully falls back to secure USSD two-factor authentication."*

### Regulator — "How do you reconcile erasure with lawful interception?"
> *"Clean Wall is our proposed architecture, reconciling both under Lex Specialis. Commercial profiles are permanently erased within 24 hours under the data protection law. Signaling metadata sits in a separate air-gapped vault for the legally required period, opened only under judicial warrant with two keys—our legal chief and our DPO."*

### Police / LEA — "Do Stealth Numbers hide criminals from the law?"
> *"Stealth Numbers shield citizens from roadside stalkers, never from lawful court warrants. The temporary MSISDN is bridged inside our network core and deterministically mapped to the caller's biometric NID in our HSS. Under a lawful warrant, law enforcement receives full unmasking in under 15 milliseconds."*

### Governance — "Is the DPO board-level veto written into the statute?"
> *"The data protection law requires an independent Data Protection Officer reporting to senior management. The binding sign-off authority over high-risk AI is our proposed internal corporate governance policy. Without it, a DPO cannot stop a risky commercial deployment."*

### Consumer Advocate — "What about unmarked shared SIMs?"
> *"Our corporate policy ensures nobody is profiled by default: marketing models run strictly on lines that affirmatively opted in. Guardian Child-Shield then provides a hard lock for parents. The trade-off is some lost marketing reach; as an executive team, we deliberately accept that trade-off."*
