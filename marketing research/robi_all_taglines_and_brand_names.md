## 1. Corporate Brand Eras & Umbrella Slogans

### AKTEL (1997 – 2010)
* **Description:** The pioneer cellular era under TM International (Bangladesh) Ltd. and NTT DoCoMo, positioned on technical engineering superiority, network reach, and transparent billing.
* **Taglines & Mottos:**
  * *"Clearly Ahead"* (Bengali: এক ধাপ এগিয়ে - *Ek Dhap Egiye*)
  * *"One Step Ahead"*
  * *"স্পষ্ট কথা"* (*Shposhto Kotha* — Clear Words / Clear Talk)
  * *"সহজ কথা"* (*Shohoj Kotha* — Simple Talk)
  * *"খুলে বলো"* (*Khule Bolo* — Speak Openly / Speak Freely)
  * *"ইউনুস... টাকা পাঠাইসি..."* (*Yunus... Taka Pathaisi...* — Iconic Easy Load TVC catchphrase)

### Robi Axiata Launch & Rebranding (2010 – 2022)
* **Description:** The corporate rebrand from AKTEL to "রবী/রবি" (The Sun) under Axiata Group Berhad, shifting focus from foreign corporate tech to deep Bengali cultural empowerment and individual human potential.
* **Taglines & Mottos:**
  * **"জ্বলে উঠুন আপন শক্তিতে"** (*Jwole Uthun Apon Shoktite* — Ignite the power within / Ignite with your own power)
  * *"রবির গান"* (*Robir Gaan* — Brand theme anthem composed by Arnob)
  * *"আপন শক্তিতে জ্বলে উঠুন বাংলাদেশ"* (*Apon shoktite jwole uthun Bangladesh*)

### Robi Resilience & Modern Era (2023 – 2026)
* **Description:** The contemporary mass-market brand posture celebrating grassroots determination (*জেদ*), economic resilience, and digital self-reliance across Bangladesh.
* **Taglines & Mottos:**
  * **"পারবে তুমিও"** (*Parbe Tumio* — You Can Too / You Can Do It Too)
  * *"Believe in You"*
  * *"Ready for Tomorrow"* / *"Ready for the Future"*

### National Cricket & Country Pride Properties
* **Description:** Long-standing sponsorship of the Bangladesh National Cricket Team (2015–2017 and 2024–2027) anchoring the brand in patriotic collective energy.
* **Taglines & Mottos:**
  * *"জানি বাংলাদেশ, পারবে তুমিও"* (*Jani Bangladesh, Parbe Tumio* — Bangladesh knows it, you can too)
  * *"চার ছক্কার হইচই"* (*Char Chhokkar Hoichoi* — Excitement of boundaries and sixes)
  * *"টাইগার্সদের সাথে রবি"* (*Tigers-der Shathe Robi* — Robi with the Tigers)

### Network Generation & Speed Campaigns (4.5G & 5G)
* **Description:** Technology infrastructure branding highlighting mobile broadband speed, low-latency streaming, and nationwide digital coverage.
* **Taglines & Mottos:**
  * **"ভিডিও স্ট্রং নেটওয়ার্ক"** (*Video Strong Network* — 4.5G Launch campaign)
  * *"লাইফ যেখানে যেভাবে, রবি ৪.৫জি সেখানে সেভাবে"* (*Life jekhane jebhabe, Robi 4.5G shekhane shebhabe* — Wherever and however life happens, Robi 4.5G is right there)
  * *"৪.৫জি সুপারনেট"* (*4.5G Supernet*)
  * *"দেশের প্রথম রবি 5G নেটওয়ার্কে স্পিডের নতুন যুগে বাংলাদেশ!"* (*Desher prothom Robi 5G network-e speed-er notun yuge Bangladesh!*)
  * *"স্পিডের নতুন যুগে আমরা"* (*Speed-er notun yuge amra* — We are in a new era of speed)

## 2. Mass-Market Prepaid & Postpaid Tariff Plans

### AKTEL Joy (জয়)
* **Description:** Partner starter pack featuring paired SIM cards with heavily discounted inter-pair calling for couples and close friends.
* **Taglines & Mottos:**
  * *"কথায় কথায় জয়"* (*Kothay Kothay Joy* — Victory in every word)
  * *"সারাদিন সারারাত জয়"* (*Saradin Sararaat Joy* — Win all day and night)

### AKTEL Phurti (ফুর্তি)
* **Description:** Promotional tariff that rewarded incoming call duration from competitor networks with bonus talktime credits.
* **Taglines & Mottos:**
  * *"ইনকামিং কলেই ফুর্তি"* (*Incoming Call-e Phurti* — Joy with every incoming call)
  * *"কথা শুনলেই বোনাস"* (*Kotha Shunlei Bonus* — Bonus for listening)

### AKTEL Dosh (দশ)
* **Description:** Flat 10-paisa/10-second pulse package introduced to dominate the late-2000s price wars.
* **Taglines & Mottos:**
  * *"দশের কথা, দশের সাথ"* (*Dosher Kotha, Dosher Saath* — Voice of the ten, company for all)
  * *"মাত্র ১০ পয়সা"* (*Matro 10 Poisha*)

### AKTEL Ekshathe (একসাথে)
* **Description:** Closed-user-group (CUG) community calling plan designed for joint families and small trading shops.
* **Taglines & Mottos:**
  * *"সবাই মিলে একসাথে"* (*Shobai Mile Ekshathe* — Everyone together as one)

### AKTEL Excellence (এক্সিলেন্স)
* **Description:** Premium corporate and high-ARPU postpaid line offering dedicated credit limits, itemized paper bills, and executive perks.
* **Taglines & Mottos:**
  * *"For those who demand excellence"*
  * *"এক্সক্লুসিভ প্রিভিলেজ"* (*Exclusive Privilege*)

### Robi Shorol (সরল - 39)
* **Description:** Flat-rate calling package offering predictable tariff pricing with an assigned *Priyo* (favourite) number.
* **Taglines & Mottos:**
  * *"সহজ হিসেব, সরল কথা"* (*Shohoj Hisheb, Shorol Kotha* — Simple calculation, straightforward talk)
  * *"কোনো প্যাঁচ নেই, একদম সরল"* (*Kono pyach nei, ekdom shorol* — No complications, completely simple)

### Robi Goti (গতি - 36)
* **Description:** Per-second billing tariff bundled with automated recharge-triggered data incentives.
* **Taglines & Mottos:**
  * *"কথায় ও কাজে গতি"* (*Kothay o Kaje Goti* — Speed in word and action)
  * *"প্রতি সেকেন্ডে গতি"* (*Proti Second-e Goti* — Speed in every second)

### Robi Nobanno (নবান্ন - 37)
* **Description:** Seasonal rural-themed tariff pack featuring discounted late-night and off-peak calling slabs.
* **Taglines & Mottos:**
  * *"নবান্নের খুশিতে মাতুক রবি"* (*Nobanner khushite matuk Robi* — Rejoice with Robi in harvest joy)

### Robi Mohotshob (মহোৎসব) Campaigns
* **Description:** High-octane seasonal recharge festivals (Recharge Mohotshob, Kotipoti Utshob) awarding cars, gold, and smartphones for daily airtime recharges.
* **Taglines & Mottos:**
  * *"রিচার্জে মহোৎসব"* (*Recharge-e Mohotshob* — The Grand Recharge Festival)
  * *"লাখপতি / কোটিপতি উৎসব"* (*Lakhpoti / Kotipoti Utshob* — Become a millionaire festival)

### Robi Poisha Usul (পয়সা উসুল)
* **Description:** Umbrella term for aggressive rate-cutters and micro-minute bundles ensuring every fraction of a poisha delivers maximum talktime.
* **Taglines & Mottos:**
  * *"একদম পয়সা উসুল!"* (*Ekdom Poisha Usul!* — 100% Value for every penny!)
  * *"১ পয়সা/সেকেন্ড"* (*1 Poisha/Second*)

### Robi Soptahjure (সপ্তাহজুড়ে)
* **Description:** Standardized 7-day voice and internet bundles tailored to the weekly cash-flow cycles of rural merchants and gig workers.
* **Taglines & Mottos:**
  * *"সপ্তাহজুড়ে ননস্টপ কথা ও নেট"* (*Soptahjure nonstop kotha o net* — Nonstop talk and net all week long)
